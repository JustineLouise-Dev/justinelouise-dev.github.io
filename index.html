01 Story

How I got here

I started learning to code at 12, during my last year of elementary school, out of plain curiosity about how the apps and tools I used every day actually worked. What began as tinkering turned into a habit of building — first small scripts, then full applications.

By 2020, I started publishing projects for other people to actually use, not just personal experiments. Since then I've worked across full-stack development and automation scripting, with a strong preference for building things that are reliable enough for someone else to depend on.
